## Ask our docs

Use the keyboard shortcut <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> (macOS) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> (Windows/Linux). This starts a chat with our AI assistant trained on our documentation.

## Watch video tutorials

Visit our [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@GetMintlify/videos) channel for tutorials and guides on using Mintlify.

## Message support

Send us a message from the [Support](https://app.mintlify.com/support) page of your dashboard. To open it, click <Icon icon="circle-question-mark" /> **Get help** at the bottom of the navigation bar, then click **Support**. You can also find **Support** in the organization section of your settings.

Click **Contact support** to open a new support case.

By default, only you can see a case you submit. To share it with your organization, turn on **Visible to your organization** before you submit. The dashboard remembers your choice for your next case.

### Track your cases

The **Cases** table lists the cases you submitted and the cases other members shared with your organization. Each case shows its subject, status, who submitted it, its visibility, and when it was last updated. Use the status filter to show **Open**, **Pending**, or **Closed** cases.

Click a case to open its conversation with our support team. From there, you can:

- Read replies from our support team.
- Send a reply, with attachments if needed.
- Change who can see the case with the **Visible to your organization** toggle. Only the person who submitted the case can change its visibility.

<Info>
  We aim to respond to all messages within 24 hours, but delays may occur during busy times.
</Info>

## Contact via email

If you can't access your dashboard, email us at <a href="mailto:support@mintlify.com">support@mintlify.com</a>. Include the URL of your [dashboard](https://app.mintlify.com/) with your organization and project in your email so we can help you faster. For example, `app.mintlify.com/your-org/your-project`.
