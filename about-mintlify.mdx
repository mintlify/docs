Mintlify is a documentation platform that helps teams create, maintain, and publish documentation that serves both people and AI tools. You write content in MDX, and Mintlify turns it into a fast, searchable, and beautifully designed documentation site.

## How Mintlify works

Mintlify follows a docs-as-code approach. Your documentation lives as MDX files in a Git repository, and a `docs.json` file controls your site's navigation, theme, and settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your content">
    Edit pages in the web editor, in your IDE, or with the [CLI](/cli/index) for local previews. Use built-in components like cards, tabs, and code groups to structure your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push your changes">
    Mintlify syncs with your GitHub or GitLab repository. Every change goes through your normal review process, so docs stay versioned alongside your code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy automatically">
    Mintlify builds and deploys your site when you merge changes to your deploy branch. Your docs are hosted on a global CDN, with support for custom domains.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Built for people and AI

More readers now reach documentation through AI tools than through a browser. Mintlify serves both audiences. Every site hosts an `llms.txt` file, a `skill.md` file, and an MCP server, so AI tools can read your content accurately. Pages are also available as Markdown by appending `.md` to the URL. Learn more in [AI-native documentation](/ai-native).

## Who uses Mintlify

Thousands of companies, from early-stage startups to large enterprises, use Mintlify for product documentation, API references, help centers, and internal knowledge bases.
