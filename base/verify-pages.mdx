Verify the pages in your base to show your team which pages are current. Add properties, such as an owner, to record who is responsible for a page and other details about it.

Verification and properties are stored in each page's frontmatter. AI tools that read your base through the [admin MCP](/ai/mintlify-mcp) or your Git repository see them along with the page content.

Verification and properties are available in every base. They aren't available in docs projects.

## Verification statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Verified** | A teammate confirmed that the page is current. |
| **Outdated** | A teammate marked the page as out of date. |
| **Expired** | The page was verified, but its verification period ended. |
| **Not verified** | No one has verified the page, or a teammate removed its verification. |

## Verify a page

To verify a page or change its status, you need the editor or admin role. For a private page, you need the **Editor** or **Full Access** role on the page. Anyone who can open a page can see its status.

1. Open the page in the editor.
2. Hover above the page title and click **Add verification**.
3. Choose how long the verification lasts: **For 90 days**, **For 30 days**, **For 7 days**, **Indefinitely**, or **Pick a date**.

The page's status appears in the top bar. Click the status to see who last verified the page or marked it outdated.

## Change a page's status

Click the status in the top bar, then:

- To change when the verification ends, use **Expiration**.
- To flag a verified page as out of date, click **Mark Outdated**.
- To verify an outdated or expired page again, choose how long the new verification lasts.
- To return the page to **Not verified**, click **Remove verification**. If the page is outdated or expired, click **Reset status**.

## Verification in your inbox

When a teammate verifies a page, marks it outdated, or removes its verification, or when a page's verification expires, these people get an item in their [inbox](/dashboard/inbox#sections-and-filters):

- The page's [owner](#add-properties). If a private page has no owner, the person who created the page gets the item instead.
- The person who last verified the page, if the page is marked outdated, has its verification removed, or expires.

If no one matches, the person who last edited the page gets the item. You don't get items for your own changes.

## Add properties

A page can have an owner and any number of custom properties.

- **Owner**: The teammate responsible for the page. The owner gets [inbox items](#verification-in-your-inbox) when the page's verification status changes.
- **Custom properties**: Fields you name yourself, such as a launch date or a team. Choose from these types: Text, Number, Date, Person, Select, Multi-select, URL, and Checkbox.

To add a property, you need the same access as for [verifying a page](#verify-a-page).

1. Open the page in the editor.
2. Hover above the page title and click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **Page properties**.
3. Select **Owner** and choose a teammate, or select **New property** and choose a type.

Properties with a value appear below the page title. You can also view and add properties in the **Properties** section of the **Page settings** panel.

## Verification and properties in Git

The editor writes a page's verification status to the `verification` frontmatter key, and its owner and properties to the `metadata` key.

```yaml
---
title: "Deploy runbook"
verification:
  status: "verified"
  expiresAt: "2027-02-01T00:00:00Z"
---
```

To set a page's status from Git, set `verification.status` to `verified`, `outdated`, or `not_verified`. For a verified page, `expiresAt` sets when the verification ends, as an ISO 8601 date and time. If you leave out `expiresAt`, the page stays verified indefinitely.

If you set a status in Git, the editor doesn't show who verified the page, and no one gets an inbox item.

The `metadata` key refers to teammates by their Mintlify user IDs. Edit owners and properties in the editor.
