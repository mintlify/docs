Verify the pages in your knowledge base to show your team which pages are current. Add properties, such as an owner, to record who is responsible for a page and other details about it.

Each page's frontmatter stores its verification status and properties. AI tools that read your knowledge base through the [MCP server](/ai/mintlify-mcp) or your Git repository see the verification status and other properties along with the page content.

Verification and properties are only available for pages in a knowledge base. They aren't available in sites.

## Verification statuses

{/* vale off */}

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Verified** | Someone confirmed that the page is current and the content is correct. |
| **Outdated** | Someone marked the page as out of date. |
| **Expired** | The page was verified, but its verification period ended. |
| **Not verified** | No one verified the page or someone removed its verification. |

{/* vale on */}

## Verify a page

To verify a teamspace page or change its status, you must have the editor or admin [role](/dashboard/roles). To verify a private page, you must have the **Editor** or **Full Access** role on the page. Anyone who can open a page can see its status.

1. Open the page in the editor.
2. Hover above the page title to display the <Icon icon="badge-check" /> **Add verification** button.
3. Click **Add verification**.
4. Choose how long the verification lasts: **For 90 days**, **For 30 days**, **For 7 days**, **Indefinitely**, or **Pick a date**.

The page's verification status appears in the top bar. Click the status to see who last verified the page or marked it outdated.

## Change a page's status

Click the verification status in the top bar to change its status.

- To change when the verification ends, use the **Expiration** menu to select a new date.
- To flag a verified page as out of date, click **Mark Outdated**.
- To verify an outdated or expired page again, choose how long the new verification lasts.
- To return the page to **Not verified**, click **Remove verification**.

## Verification in your inbox

When a teammate verifies a page, marks it outdated, or removes its verification, or when a page's verification expires, Mintlify sends a notification to the [inbox](/dashboard/inbox#sections-and-filters) of the page's [owner](#add-properties) and the person who last verified the page if the page is marked outdated, has its verification removed, or expires.

You don't receive notifications for your own changes.

## Add properties

A page can have an owner and any number of custom properties.

- **Owner**: The teammate responsible for the page. The owner gets inbox notifications when the page's verification status changes.
- **Custom properties**: Fields you name yourself, such as a launch date or a team. Choose from these types: Text, Number, Date, Person, Select, Multi-select, URL, and Checkbox.

To add a property, you must have the editor or admin [role](/dashboard/roles). To add properties to a private page, you must have the **Editor** or **Full Access** role on the page. Anyone who can open a page can see its properties.

1. Open the page in the editor.
2. Hover above the page title to display the <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **Page properties** menu.
3. To add an owner, select **Owner** and choose a teammate.
4. To add a custom property, select **New property** and choose a type.

Properties with a value appear below the page title. You can also view and add properties in the **Properties** section of the **Page settings** panel.

## Verification and properties in Git

The editor writes a page's verification status to the `verification` frontmatter key, and its owner and properties to the `metadata` key.

```yaml Example verification frontmatter
---
title: "Deploy runbook"
verification:
  status: "verified"
  expiresAt: "2027-02-01T00:00:00Z"
---
```

To change a page's status by editing the file, set `verification.status` to `verified`, `outdated`, or `not_verified`. For a verified page, set `expiresAt` to when the verification ends as an ISO 8601 date and time. If you omit `expiresAt`, the page remains verified indefinitely.

If you change a page's status by editing the file, the editor doesn't show who verified the page and no one receives an inbox notification.

The `metadata` key refers to teammates by their Mintlify user IDs. Edit owners and properties in the editor.
