Mintlify Base is a knowledge base for your team and the agents they work with. Keep specs, runbooks, onboarding guides, and other internal knowledge in a base, and connect it to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and other AI tools through MCP so agents work from the same information your team does.

A base uses the same [editor](/editor/index) and [agent](/editor/agent) as your docs. If you already know how to write pages in Mintlify, you know how to work in a base.

## How a base differs from docs

Docs and bases are separate projects in your organization. The project switcher in your dashboard lists them under **Docs** and **Base**.

|  | Docs | Base |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Your customers and their agents | Your team and its agents |
| Where people read it | A docs site, public or behind [authentication](/deploy/authentication-setup) | Your dashboard and connected AI tools, for members of your organization only |
| Saving changes | Edit on a branch, then publish | Edits to shared pages commit to your repository automatically |
| Navigation | Tabs, anchors, groups, versions, and languages | Pages and groups |

Because a base has no docs site, settings that only apply to a site, such as custom domains, the assistant, and analytics, aren't available for a base.

## Where your pages live

The editor sidebar in a base has two kinds of pages.

- **Teamspace**: Pages your whole organization shares. Mintlify stores Teamspace pages as MDX files in a Git repository and commits your edits automatically.
- **Personal** and **Shared with me**: [Private pages](/editor/pages#private-pages) for your drafts. Private pages aren't stored in Git. They stay private to you until you share them with teammates. When a draft is ready, open its options menu and select **Move to Teamspace**.

New bases use a repository that Mintlify hosts for you. To work on your base locally with your own tools, connect a GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, or Azure Repos repository in your [Git settings](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/project/git-settings). Commits you push to the connected repository update your base.

## How people and agents contribute

Your team can update a base wherever they work:

- **Editor**: Write and organize pages in your browser or the [desktop app](/desktop), with help from the [agent](/editor/agent).
- **MCP**: Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another AI tool to the [admin MCP](/ai/mintlify-mcp) to read, search, and edit your base from a conversation.
- **Git**: Clone your base's repository and edit the MDX files with any editor or coding agent.

## Share pages with your docs

To reuse a base page in your docs, open its options menu and select **Copy to a project**, then select your docs project. The copy is independent of the original, so later edits to one don't change the other. Publish the copy from your docs project to make it live.
