## Create your base

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="New to Mintlify">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign up for Mintlify">
        Go to [app.mintlify.com/signup?product=base](https://app.mintlify.com/signup?product=base) and create your account. Then click **Get started**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose how you'll make updates">
        Select how you plan to make updates: **Web editor**, **Local development**, or **MCP**. This selection is only for the initial setup. You can use every method in your base later.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create the project">
        Click **Create project**. Your base opens in the editor with a starter page.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Existing organization">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a base from the project switcher in your dashboard">
        1. In your [dashboard](https://app.mintlify.com/), click the project switcher at the top of the left sidebar.
        2. Click <Icon icon="plus" /> **New project**.
        3. Select **Base**, then click **Continue**.

        Your base is part of the same organization as your other projects.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose how you'll make updates">
        Select how you plan to make updates: **Web editor**, **Local development**, or **MCP**. This selection is only for the initial setup. You can use every method in your base later.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create the project">
        Click **Create project**. Your base opens in the editor with a starter page.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Check>
  Your base appears under **Base** in the project switcher in your dashboard. The editor sidebar shows a setup checklist with your next steps.
</Check>

## Add pages

Create pages for your team in the **Teamspace** section of the sidebar.

Click the <Icon icon="plus" /> plus button beside the **Teamspace** header and select **New page**. To bring in existing Markdown files, drag them from your computer onto the sidebar.

When you make changes to pages in your Teamspace, Mintlify commits them to your base's repository automatically. To draft privately first, create a page in the **Personal** section and move it to your **Teamspace** when you want it to be available to your team.

For more information on editing pages, see [Create and edit pages](/editor/pages).

## Invite your team

Members of your organization can access your base. To add teammates, invite them from your [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) settings.

## Connect your AI tools

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another AI tool to your base so it can search, read, and update your pages.

1. In your dashboard, open your base and go to the [MCP](https://app.mintlify.com/products/mcp) settings.
2. Under **Connect**, select your AI tool and follow the prompts to sign in.

When an AI tool edits your base, it commits changes the same way the editor does. For the tools available and example prompts, see [Admin MCP](/ai/mintlify-mcp).

## (Optional) Connect your own repository

Your base starts with a repository that Mintlify hosts. To edit your base locally, connect a repository from your [Git settings](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/project/git-settings). Commits pushed to the connected repository update your base.
