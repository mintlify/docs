Create a base, add your team, and connect the AI tools you already use. For an overview of how a base works, see [Mintlify Base](/base/index).

## Prerequisites

- A Mintlify account. If you don't have one, you create one when you start setup.

If your organization is on an Enterprise plan, [contact sales](https://mintlify.com/enterprise) to add a base.

## Create your base

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start setup">
    Go to [app.mintlify.com/onboarding?product=base](https://app.mintlify.com/onboarding?product=base) and sign in or create an account.

    If your organization already has a Mintlify project, your base is added to the same organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you'll make updates">
    On the welcome screen, click **Get started**. Select the way you plan to edit: **Web editor**, **Local development**, or **MCP**.

    Your choice only affects onboarding. You can use every method in your base at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the project">
    Click **Create project**. Your base opens in the editor with a starter page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your base appears under **Base** in the project switcher in your dashboard.
</Check>

## Add pages

Create pages for your team in the **Teamspace** section of the sidebar. Click the <Icon icon="plus" /> plus button on **Teamspace** and select **New page**. To bring in existing Markdown, drag `.md` and `.mdx` files from your computer onto the sidebar.

Teamspace edits commit to your base's repository automatically. To draft privately first, create the page in **Personal** and move it to **Teamspace** when it's ready. For more on editing, see [Create and edit pages](/editor/pages).

## Invite your team

Members of your organization can open your base. To add teammates, invite them from your [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) settings.

## Connect your AI tools

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another AI tool to your base so it can search, read, and update your pages.

1. In your dashboard, open your base and go to [MCP](https://app.mintlify.com/products/mcp).
2. Under **Connect**, select your AI tool and follow the prompts to sign in.

When the AI tool edits your base, it commits changes the same way the editor does. For the tools available and example prompts, see [Admin MCP](/ai/mintlify-mcp).

## Connect your own repository

Optional: your base starts with a repository that Mintlify hosts. To edit your base locally with Git, the [CLI](/cli/index), or a coding agent, connect a repository from your [Git settings](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/project/git-settings). Commits pushed to the connected repository update your base.
