<Info>
  Bring your own model requires an [Enterprise plan](https://mintlify.com/pricing?ref=bring-your-own-model) and is a separate line item on your contract. [Contact sales](https://www.mintlify.com/contact/sales) to add it. The setting does not appear in your dashboard until Mintlify enables it for your deployment.
</Info>

By default, Mintlify selects and pays for the models behind its AI products. Bring your own model replaces that with your own provider account: you supply an API key, you choose the model, and inference runs against your provider.

Use it to standardize on a specific model, keep AI traffic with a provider you already have an agreement with, or reduce the credits that Mintlify's AI products consume.

One configuration covers every AI product on the deployment: the [assistant](/assistant), the [agent](/agent), and [automations](/automations/index).

## Supported providers

| Provider | Coverage |
|:---------|:---------|
| OpenRouter | Full. Every AI product uses your key and your model. |
| Anthropic | Limited. Some AI products continue to use Mintlify's models. |
| OpenAI-compatible | Limited. Some AI products continue to use Mintlify's models. |

Choose OpenRouter unless you have a reason not to. It is the only provider with complete coverage across the assistant, the agent, and automations. The dashboard marks the other two options as limited and warns you when you select one.

The OpenAI-compatible option accepts any provider that exposes an OpenAI-style API. You supply a provider name and base URL alongside your key.

Each provider supports a fixed set of models. The **Model** dropdown lists the models available for the provider you select.

## Configure your model

Editors and admins can configure the model. See [Roles and permissions](/dashboard/roles).

1. Go to the [API keys](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/api-keys) page in your dashboard.
2. In the **AI model** section, enable **Bring your own model**.
3. Select a provider. For an OpenAI-compatible provider, enter a provider name and base URL.
4. Select a model.
5. Enter your provider API key.
6. Select **Save**.

Mintlify stores your key encrypted and never displays it again after you save it.

## How credits work

Mintlify's AI products still consume [credits](/credits) when you bring your own model, but at a reduced rate.

The reduced rate applies to assistant messages on any supported provider. For agent runs and automations, it applies only when your provider is OpenRouter.

Your provider bills you directly for inference. Those charges do not appear in your Mintlify usage, so set any spending limits with your provider.

## Rotate or remove your key

Mintlify reuses your saved key across models for the same provider. Enter a key again only to rotate it or when you switch providers.

To rotate your key, enter the new key in the **API key** field and select **Save**.

To stop using your own model, disable **Bring your own model**. Mintlify deletes the stored configuration and key, and AI products return to Mintlify's default models at the standard credit rate.
