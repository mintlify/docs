Your users ask AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor about your product. Mintlify hosts files and endpoints on your site that give those tools accurate, current answers from your documentation. On a public site, you don't need to set any of them up.

To give your own team's AI tools write access to your content, use the [admin MCP](/ai/mintlify-mcp) instead.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Search MCP server" icon="plug" href="/ai/model-context-protocol">
    Let users connect AI tools to your site so the tools can search and read your pages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="llms.txt" icon="list" href="/ai/llmstxt">
    Give AI tools an index of every page, and the full text of your docs in one file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="skill.md" icon="wand-sparkles" href="/ai/skillmd">
    Describe what agents can do with your product, generated from your docs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Markdown export" icon="file-text" href="/ai/markdown-export">
    Serve a Markdown version of any page by adding `.md` to its URL.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If your site requires authentication, some of these behave differently. See [Feature availability](/deploy/authentication-setup#feature-availability).
