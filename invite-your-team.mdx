Invite the people who write, review, and manage your documentation so they can work with you in the [editor](/editor) and dashboard.

## Before you begin

- **Permission to invite**: If your organization doesn't use [roles](#how-permissions-work), any member can invite teammates. With roles, check that your role can invite the role you want to give. See [Roles](#roles).
- **Member limit**: Your plan sets how many members your organization can have. When you reach it, the **Members** page shows a notice and turns off the invite form.

## Invite teammates

1. Open the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In **New member**, enter your teammates' email addresses. Separate addresses with commas, semicolons, spaces, or new lines.
3. If your organization uses roles, choose a role for the new members.
4. Click **Invite**.

<Tip>
  You can also start from anywhere in the dashboard. Click your project icon at the top of the sidebar and select **Invite members**, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (macOS) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (Windows) and select **Invite members**. Then continue from step 2.
</Tip>

Mintlify emails each person an invitation. They join your organization when they accept it.

On paid plans, you can also [upload a CSV file](/dashboard/members#add-members-to-your-organization) to invite many people at once. To invite teammates from an AI tool, use the `manage_members_sharing` tool in the [admin MCP](/ai/mintlify-mcp#project-management).

### Manage pending invites

Invites that teammates haven't accepted yet appear in the **Invites** section of the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page. Open an invite's actions menu and select:

- **Resend invite** to send the invitation email again.
- **Cancel invite** to revoke the invitation. The person can no longer join with that invite link.

## How permissions work

Without roles, every member has full access to your organization's dashboard, and new members join as editors.

With roles, each member has one role in your organization. Their role controls what they can do in the dashboard and editor.

### Roles

<Info>
  Role-based access control (RBAC) requires an [Enterprise plan](https://mintlify.com/pricing?ref=rbac).
</Info>

Organizations with roles have three standard roles:

- **Viewer**: Views content, the dashboard, and analytics, and leaves comments. Can't edit.
- **Editor**: Creates, edits, and publishes content, and configures integrations and the assistant.
- **Admin**: Everything an editor can do, plus managing members, roles, billing, security, the custom domain, and the Git source.

If the role options on your **Members** page include **Publisher**, your organization uses [expanded roles](/dashboard/roles#expanded-roles): owner, admin, publisher, editor, commenter, and viewer. Expanded roles separate editing from publishing, so editors request a review and publishers approve and publish. Organizations with expanded roles can also create [custom roles](/dashboard/roles#custom-roles).

Give each person the narrowest role they need. See [Roles](/dashboard/roles) for the full list of permissions for each role.

Who can invite depends on your roles:

- **Standard roles**: Editors and admins can invite editors and viewers. Only admins can invite admins.
- **Expanded roles**: Owners and admins can invite members. You can only invite members with a role up to your own.

To change a member's role later, see [Change a member's role](/dashboard/members#change-a-members-role).

### Members from your identity provider

If your organization uses [SSO](/dashboard/sso), people who sign in with SSO for the first time join your organization without an invite. They get the role your identity provider assigns them, or join as editors if it doesn't assign one.

If you use [SCIM](/dashboard/scim), your identity provider adds and removes members and sets their roles. Change those members' roles in your identity provider, not in Mintlify.

### Repository access

Dashboard roles only control what people can do in the Mintlify dashboard and editor. Anyone with write access to your documentation repository can push changes, and pushes to your deploy branch deploy automatically, whatever their dashboard role. Manage repository access in your Git provider.
