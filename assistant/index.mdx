The assistant is an AI chat on your documentation site. Readers ask questions in natural language and get answers from your content, so they can succeed with your product even when they don't know where to look.

## What readers can do

- **Get answers with sources.** The assistant cites the pages it used and links readers straight to them.
- **Ask about your API.** Answers include methods, parameters, request bodies, and response schemas from your OpenAPI specifications.
- **Copy code examples.** The assistant writes code examples based on your documentation that readers can copy.
- **Add context.** Readers can attach images and files, highlight text, or send a code block to the assistant. The assistant also knows which page the reader is on.
- **Reach your team.** When the assistant can't answer, it hands the reader off to your support or sales team.

See [Use the assistant](/assistant/use) for every way readers can open the assistant.

## What the assistant can access

The assistant answers only from your published documentation and API specifications, plus any [search domains](/assistant/configure#search-domains) you add. If it can't find relevant information, it says so instead of guessing.

When you publish changes, the assistant indexes new, updated, or deleted content right away. It does not index draft branches or preview deployments.

By default, the assistant does not index hidden pages. To include hidden pages in the assistant's index, set `seo.indexing: "all"` in your `docs.json`. See [Hidden pages](/organize/hidden-pages#search-seo-and-ai-indexing) for more information.

## Improve the assistant's answers

The assistant answers best when your content is easy to find and understand. Clear organization and comprehensive context benefit both human readers and AI understanding.

- Write descriptive headings and specific frontmatter descriptions for each page.
- Define terms and acronyms when you first introduce them.
- Include examples and use cases, and cross-reference related topics.
- Add [hidden pages](/organize/hidden-pages) with context that readers don't need, but the assistant can reference.
- Use the [`Visibility`](/components/visibility) component to show agent-only context to the assistant without cluttering your published pages.
