The assistant is an AI chat on your documentation site. Users ask questions in natural language and get answers from your content, so they can succeed with your product even when they don't know where to look.

## What your users can do with the assistant

- Get answers that cite the pages they come from, with links to each page.
- Ask about your API and get methods, parameters, request bodies, and response schemas from your OpenAPI specifications.
- Copy code examples that the assistant writes from your documentation.
- Ask about screenshots and diagrams on your pages.
- Add context by highlighting text, sending a code block, or attaching files. The assistant also knows which page the user is on. See [Add context to a question](/assistant/use#add-context-to-a-question).
- Reach your team by email, your help center, or a support ticket when the assistant can't answer.

## How the assistant answers

The assistant answers only from your published documentation and API specifications, plus any [search domains](/assistant/sources#search-domains) you add. If it can't find relevant information, it says so instead of guessing. See [Manage what the assistant knows](/assistant/sources) to control which content it uses.
