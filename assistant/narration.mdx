<Info>
  Narration requires a [Pro or Enterprise plan](https://mintlify.com/pricing).
</Info>

Narration adds a **Listen to page** control on each page. When a reader clicks **Listen to page**, Mintlify generates a spoken version of the page and streams the audio into the browser.

Mintlify generates narration on demand from your published content, so audio always matches the content of the page. When a page changes, the next listener triggers a fresh generation.

The **Listen to page** button only appears on pages with narratable content. Mintlify cannot narrate very short pages or pages that are mostly non-prose. Narration is not available for deployments that require authentication to access.

Narration uses credits from your organization's shared credit balance. Track usage on the [Usage](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/usage) page of your dashboard.

## Enable narration

1. Navigate to the **Narration** section of the [General](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/deployment/general) settings in your dashboard.
2. Enable **Page narration**.
