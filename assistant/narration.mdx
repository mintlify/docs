## About narration

Narration adds a **Listen to page** control near the top of each page. When a reader activates it, Mintlify generates a spoken version of the page with ElevenLabs, streams the audio into the browser, and highlights the sentence being spoken while it scrolls into view.

Mintlify generates narration on demand from your published content, so audio always matches the page a reader is looking at. When a page changes, the next listener triggers a fresh generation.

The Listen pill only appears on pages with narratable content. Very short pages and pages that are mostly non-prose are skipped automatically.

## Enable narration

Prerequisites:

- The assistant add-on is enabled on your deployment.
- Your documentation site does not require end-user authentication. Narration is not served on auth-gated deployments.

To enable narration:

1. In your dashboard, go to **Settings** > **Docs audio**.
2. Turn on **Read aloud**.
3. Select a **Voice** from the ElevenLabs voice picker. The same voice is used for every page.

The Listen pill appears on your live documentation site after you save.

## How readers use it

- **Start**: Click **Listen to page** near the page title. Mintlify streams the audio while it is being generated, so playback starts within a few seconds.
- **Pause and resume**: Click the pill again to pause. Click once more to resume from the same position.
- **Follow along**: The current sentence is highlighted and the page auto-scrolls to keep it in view. Collapsed accordions and expandables open as they are read.
- **Navigate away**: Moving to another page stops narration for the previous page.

Narration honors the reader's selected locale. If a page is not available in that locale, Mintlify falls back to the source language.

## Billing

Narration usage draws from your organization's shared credit balance, the same balance that powers the assistant, the agent, and automations. Track usage on the [Usage](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/usage) page.
