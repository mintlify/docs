Narration adds a **Listen to this page** player at the top of each page. When a reader presses play, Mintlify generates a spoken version of the page and streams the audio into the browser.

Mintlify generates narration on demand from your published content, so audio always matches the content of the page. When a page changes, the next listener triggers a fresh generation.

The **Listen to this page** player only appears on pages with narratable content. See [Which pages you can narrate](#which-pages-you-can-narrate) for details.

Narration uses credits from your organization's shared credit balance. Each generation costs 50 credits. Replays of the same page version are free. See [Credit pricing](/credits) for details. Track usage on the [Usage](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/usage) page of your dashboard.

## How narration works

Narration reads the page title, description, and body in order. Mintlify announces steps, tabs, callouts, and accordions with short spoken cues. Narration skips code blocks, tables, media, and API fields.

Mintlify replaces [variables](/organize/settings-reference#variables) with their values before narrating, so readers hear the same text they see on the page.

While audio plays, Mintlify highlights the sentence being read and scrolls to keep it in view. If a reader scrolls away, automatic scrolling stops and the audio continues. Opening another page stops the current narration.

### Playback controls

The player shows a play and pause button, the elapsed time, the total duration, and a playback speed button.

Before anyone generates audio for a page, the player shows an estimated duration based on the page's word count. After the first generation, the player shows the exact duration of the recording.

Each click on the speed button cycles through the available speeds in this order: 1.0x, 1.2x, 1.5x, 2.0x, and 0.8x. Playback starts at 1.0x.

### Languages

Mintlify narrates each page in the language of the [language division](/organize/navigation#languages) that contains it. If your navigation doesn't define languages, Mintlify narrates in English.

## Which pages you can narrate

Mintlify shows the player only when a page has enough prose to narrate:

- **Minimum length:** The narratable text must contain at least 10 words. For languages that don't separate words with spaces, such as Chinese and Japanese, the text must contain at least 40 characters.
- **Maximum length:** Pages with more than 40,000 characters of narratable text don't show the player.
- **Non-prose content:** Narration skips code blocks, tables, media, and API fields, so pages made up mostly of this content often fall below the minimum length.

Narration is not available for projects that use any of the following:

- [Authentication](/deploy/authentication-setup) or [personalization](/create/personalization)
- An assistant configured to [bring your own model](/ai/bring-your-own-model)

The **Narration** settings section doesn't appear in your dashboard for these projects. Narration also doesn't run on [preview deployments](/deploy/preview-deployments).

## Enable narration

1. Navigate to the **Narration** section of the [General](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/project/general) settings in your dashboard.
2. Enable **Page narration**.

To stop showing the player, disable **Page narration**. If you don't have permission to change the setting, contact an admin on your team.
