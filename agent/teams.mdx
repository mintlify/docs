<Note>
  The Teams agent is rolling out gradually. If it isn't enabled for your organization, the bot tells you so. Contact [support@mintlify.com](mailto:support@mintlify.com) to request access.
</Note>

Use the agent in Microsoft Teams to update your documentation. Mention `@Mintlify` in a Teams channel or group chat and describe the docs change you need. The agent reads your docs, drafts the edit, and opens a pull request for your team to review.

## Requirements

- A Mintlify account with a connected docs repository. Some features may require a paid Mintlify plan.
- Each person's Microsoft account email must match their Mintlify account email. Otherwise, the bot can't match them to a Mintlify user.

## Connect your Teams tenant

<Note>
  Each Teams tenant can connect to one Mintlify organization.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Mintlify app">
    In Microsoft Teams, go to **Apps** and search for "Mintlify." Add the app.

    Your Teams admin may need to allow the app in the Teams admin center first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app to a team or group chat">
    Add the app to a team or group chat where you want to use it. The bot posts a welcome message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Mintlify organization">
    Send the Mintlify bot a direct message. It replies with a connect card. Click it, sign in to Mintlify, and choose the organization to link.

    Connect links are personal and expire after 15 minutes. If your link expires, send the bot another direct message to get a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a request">
    Go back to a channel and mention `@Mintlify` with your request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Disconnect the agent

Admins can review or disconnect the Teams connection from the Mintlify dashboard.

Removing the app from your personal scope in Teams deletes your personal chat history with the bot.

## Update documentation

- **New request**: Mention `@Mintlify` in a channel or group chat and describe the docs change you need.
- **Existing thread**: Reply in the same thread and mention `@Mintlify` again to iterate on the change.

The bot only sees messages that mention it. It does not read other messages in your channels. If the bot doesn't respond to a reply, make sure your message mentions `@Mintlify`.

## Get help

Mention `@Mintlify` with `help` at any time, or type `help` in a direct message, to see what the agent can do.

## Direct messages

Direct messages are currently used for connecting your organization and for help. Docs requests happen by mentioning `@Mintlify` in a channel or group chat. If you send a request in a direct message, the bot points you to a channel.

## Privacy

For details on how Mintlify handles data from Microsoft Teams, see the Microsoft Teams Integration section of the [privacy policy](https://www.mintlify.com/legal/privacy).

## Best practices

- **Be specific**: Tell the agent exactly what you want changed and where.
- **Add context**: Include the details the agent needs in the message that mentions `@Mintlify`, since the bot doesn't read other messages in the channel.
- **Review carefully**: Always review pull requests that the agent creates before merging them.
