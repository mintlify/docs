keywords: ["databases", "records", "sort", "cursor"]
---

<Info>
  Databases are in early access and must be enabled for your project. Contact your Mintlify account team to turn it on.
</Info>

Use this endpoint to page through every record. To filter or count, use [Query records](/api/databases/query-records).

`sort` takes a column name with an optional direction, such as `sort=Release date:desc`. URL-encode the space. Pass `nextCursor` as `cursor` to get the next page; it is `null` on the last page.
