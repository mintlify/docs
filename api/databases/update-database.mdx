<Info>
  Databases are in early access and must be enabled for your project. Contact your Mintlify account team to turn it on.
</Info>

Renaming a database changes its `name` only. The slug changes only when you send `slug`, and the old slug then stops resolving, so update anything that addresses the database by slug first.
