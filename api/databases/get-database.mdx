<Info>
  Databases are in early access and must be enabled for your project. Contact your Mintlify account team to turn it on.
</Info>

The response includes every column in display order and a change `cursor` as of this read. Pass that cursor to [List changes](/api/databases/list-changes) or a [live updates session](/databases/live-updates) to pick up every change made after the read.
