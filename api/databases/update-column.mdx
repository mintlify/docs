<Info>
  Databases are in early access and must be enabled for your project. Contact your Mintlify account team to turn it on.
</Info>

Address the column by its name, in any case, or by its `col_` id. Renaming a column keeps every record's value; records return the value under the new name. A column's type cannot change.
