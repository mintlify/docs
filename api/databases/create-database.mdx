<Info>
  Databases are in early access and must be enabled for your project. Contact your Mintlify account team to turn it on.
</Info>

The slug is derived from `name` when you omit it: `App versions` becomes `app_versions`. Address the database by its slug in every other request. See [addressing](/databases/api#addressing).

Send an `Idempotency-Key` to make retries safe. See [idempotency](/databases/api#idempotency).
