Choose which emails Mintlify sends you from the [Notifications](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/notifications) page of your dashboard. Notification preferences apply to your account only. Each member of your organization manages their own preferences.

All notification types are on by default. Turn off any you don't want to receive.

## Email notification types

Turn emails on or off for each event.

| Category | Events |
| -------- | ------ |
| Comments | New comments on pages you own, replies to threads you follow, `@` mentions, thread resolutions, and reactions to your comments. See [Collaborate in the editor](/editor/collaborate). |
| Suggestions | Suggestions submitted, accepted, and rejected. |
| Automation runs | Succeeded and failed [automation](/automations/index) runs. If a run fails, Mintlify pauses further failure emails for that automation until a run succeeds again. |
| Page shares | A teammate shares a page with you. |
| General product feedback | Positive and negative [page feedback](/optimize/feedback). |

<Note>
  Notifications are email only. The Notifications page shows a Slack column for each event, but Slack delivery isn't available yet.
</Note>

## Organization contacts

Admins can manage organization-wide contact emails in the Organization contacts section.

- **Security contact email**: Available on Enterprise plans. An email address that receives security-related communications. See [Security contact](/dashboard/security-contact) for more information.
- **Admin contact emails**: One or more email addresses that receive billing alerts, deployment notifications, and other communications about your organization.
