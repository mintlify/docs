<Info>
  SSO requires an [Enterprise plan](https://mintlify.com/pricing?ref=sso).
</Info>

Admins set up SSO from the [Security](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/access) page of the dashboard. Each setup button opens the admin portal, which walks you through the configuration for your identity provider.

To set up SSO, first verify your email domain, then connect your identity provider.

## Verify your domain

Before you set up SSO, verify ownership of your email domains. After you connect SSO, anyone who signs in with an email address on a verified domain goes to your identity provider.

1. Navigate to the [Security](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/access) page in your dashboard.
2. In the **SSO** section, click **Verify email domains**.
3. In the admin portal, add your domain. The admin portal displays a DNS record.
4. Add the DNS record from the admin portal to your DNS provider.

After the DNS record propagates, **Verified email domains** shows a **Verified** badge on the **Security** page.

## Connect your identity provider

1. Navigate to the [Security](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/access) page in your dashboard.
2. In the **SSO** section, click **Set up SSO**. The button is unavailable until you verify an email domain.
3. In the admin portal, follow the steps for your identity provider.

When the connection is active, the **SSO** section shows **SAML SSO** with a **Connected** badge.

To change your SSO configuration later, click **Manage SSO** to reopen the admin portal.

## Sign in with SSO

Members can sign in from the [Mintlify login page](https://app.mintlify.com/login) with an email address on a verified domain or from your identity provider's app catalog.

After you connect SSO, test both sign-in flows in a private browser window before you tell your team to use SSO.

## Automatic member provisioning

When someone signs in through SSO for the first time, Mintlify adds them to your organization. New members get the [role](/dashboard/roles) that your identity provider assigns them. If your identity provider doesn't assign a role, they join as editors.

To provision and deprovision members before they sign in, set up [SCIM](/dashboard/scim).
