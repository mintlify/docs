Manage your personal details and dashboard preferences in your [Profile](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/account) settings. Profile settings apply to your account only and don't change what other members of your organization see.

## Personal information

Update your first and last name, or upload a profile picture. Profile pictures can be PNG, JPEG, WebP, or SVG files up to 4 MB.

Your email is the address you signed in with. You can't change it from your profile.

## Starting page

The dashboard opens to **Home**, which is the web editor, by default. To open a different page, select it from the **Starting page** menu in the **Preferences** section:

- **Home**
- **Activity**
- **Analytics**
- **Automations**

Mintlify saves your choice immediately.

The dashboard uses your starting page when you open the dashboard, switch organizations, or open the root URL of a project, such as `https://app.mintlify.com/{organization}/{project}`. Links to a specific page, document, or branch still open that destination.

If your starting page isn't available for a project, the dashboard opens **Home** instead. If the editor isn't available either, the dashboard opens **Activity**.

## Theme

Choose **Light** or **Dark** in the **Appearance** section. Your theme applies only to the device you choose it on.

## Workspace

The **Workspace** section shows when you joined the workspace. If your organization uses [roles](/dashboard/roles), it also shows your role.

## Connect GitHub

Click **Authorize** in the **Integrations** section to connect your GitHub account. After you connect it:

- Mintlify signs [commits you make in the web editor](/editor/publish#commit-signing) with your GitHub account instead of the Mintlify GitHub App.
- The agent attributes [pull requests it opens](/agent/index#connect-your-github-account) to you instead of the Mintlify bot.

To remove the connection, click **Disconnect**.
