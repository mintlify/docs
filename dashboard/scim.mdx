</Info>

SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) lets admins provision and deprovision Mintlify organization members directly from your identity provider. The dashboard calls this connection directory sync.

SCIM manages who has access to your organization. To control how members sign in, set up [SSO](/dashboard/sso).

## Set up directory sync

1. Navigate to the **SCIM** section of the [Security](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/access) page in your dashboard.
2. Click **Set up directory sync**.
3. In the Admin Portal, follow the steps for your identity provider.

When the connection is active, **Directory sync** shows a **Connected** badge. Your identity provider then adds members to your organization and removes them when you remove them from the Mintlify app in your identity provider.

To change your directory sync configuration later, click **Manage directory** to reopen the Admin Portal.

## Roles for provisioned members

Provisioned members get the [role](/dashboard/roles) that your identity provider assigns them. When you change a member's role assignment in your identity provider, their role in Mintlify updates to match.

On the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page, provisioned members show **Managed by SSO**. Change their roles in your identity provider instead of in the dashboard.
