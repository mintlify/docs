The inbox collects the comments, suggestions, and page shares that need your attention so you can find them without searching your email. Click <Icon icon="inbox" /> **Inbox** in the left sidebar of your dashboard to open it. A dot beside the icon means you have unread items.

The inbox shows activity for the project you have open. To see activity for another project, switch projects.

## What appears in your inbox

You get an inbox item when:

- A teammate or the Mintlify agent replies in a comment or suggestion thread you started or were mentioned in.
- Someone `@` mentions you in a comment or in [page content](/editor/collaborate#mentions-in-page-content). A new mention on a page you were already mentioned on returns its existing item to **Unread**.
- Someone comments on or suggests an edit to a page you own, such as a private page you manage or a site page you wrote most of. You also get later replies in those threads.
- Someone shares a private page or folder with you.

Each thread has one item. New activity in a thread updates its existing item and moves it to the top of the list. Your own actions never create inbox items for you.

The inbox and [email notifications](/dashboard/notifications) are separate. Your email preferences don't change what appears in your inbox.

To read your inbox from an AI tool, connect the [admin MCP](/ai/mintlify-mcp) and use the [`read_inbox` tool](/ai/mintlify-mcp#comments).

## Sections and filters

Use the sidebar to switch between sections:

- **All**: Everything in your inbox.
- **Suggestions**: Suggested edits and replies to them. Filter by **From the agent** or **From people**.
- **Comments**: Comment threads. Filter by **Mentions** or **On my pages**.
- **Invitations**: Private pages and folders teammates shared with you.

Each section shows how many items need your attention. Use the date picker to change the time range, which defaults to the last 30 days.

## Work through your inbox

Each item shows who acted, what they did, and on which page, with a preview of the latest comment or suggestion.

- Click **View** on a comment to open the thread in the editor.
- Click **Review** on a suggestion to open it in the editor, where you can accept or reject it. See [Suggestions](/editor/collaborate#suggestions) for more information.
- Click **View page** to open a page someone shared with you.
- Click **Dismiss** on a suggestion to remove it from your list. Dismissing doesn't reject the suggestion.

Opening a comment or page share moves it to **Read**. A suggestion stays in **Unread** until someone accepts or rejects it, or until you dismiss it.

Items also move to **Read** when the thread is resolved, the suggestion is accepted or rejected, or you lose access to the page. These items show a label indicating their status, such as **Resolved**, **Applied**, **Rejected**, or **Access removed**. If a thread gets new activity after its item moves to **Read**, the item returns to **Unread**.

To clear everything in the current view, click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **More inbox actions** and select **Mark all as read**.

## Find read items

In the **All** section, switch from **Unread** to **Read** to see items you opened, dismissed, or marked as read, and items that were resolved. To move an item you cleared back to **Unread**, click **Restore**.
