Manage who can access your organization's dashboard from the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page. Each member has a [role](/dashboard/roles) that controls what they can do.

## Add members to your organization

By default, the person who creates your Mintlify organization has admin access. If your organization uses [expanded roles](/dashboard/roles#expanded-roles), that person is the owner. Add additional members from the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.

<Info>
  Free plans can have up to 5 members. Pro and Enterprise plans for sites can invite any number of members.
</Info>

Editors and admins can invite editors and viewers. Only admins can invite other admins.

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In **New member**, enter email addresses, or paste a list separated by commas, semicolons, spaces, or new lines. You can invite up to 250 addresses at a time.
3. Choose a role. On Enterprise plans, the role defaults to **Viewer**.
4. Click **Invite**.

If every address is new, the invites send right away. If any addresses are invalid, duplicates, or already members or invited, a review drawer opens. Remove rows or change roles, then click **Send invites**.

To open the invite form from anywhere in the dashboard, click your project icon at the top of the sidebar and select **Invite members**. You can also [search the dashboard](/editor#search-the-dashboard) for **Invite members**.

<Info>
  Bulk invite requires a [Pro or Enterprise plan](https://mintlify.com/pricing).
</Info>

Use **Bulk invite** to upload a CSV of emails. Choose a role, click **Invite**, and review the list before you send the invites.

### Manage pending invites

Invites that people haven't accepted yet appear in the **Invites** section of the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page. Open an invite's actions menu, then:

- Select **Resend invite** to send the invitation email again.
- Select **Cancel invite** to revoke the invite, so the person can no longer join with that invite link. Only admins can cancel invites.

## Change a member's role

Admins can change the role assigned to any member.

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the member's row, open the actions menu and select **Edit role**. The **Edit access and permissions** drawer opens.
3. Under **Organization**, choose a new **Role**.
4. Click **Save**.

The new role takes effect the next time the member loads the dashboard. You can't change your own role. If a member's role comes from [SCIM directory sync](/dashboard/scim#roles-for-provisioned-members), their row shows **Managed by SSO**, and you change their role in your identity provider.

If your organization uses [expanded roles](/dashboard/roles#expanded-roles), the owner's role can only change if the owner transfers ownership to another member.

### Set a member's role on a project {#set-a-members-role-on-a-project}

If your organization uses [expanded roles](/dashboard/roles#expanded-roles) and has more than one project, you can give a member more access on one project or block them from it. See [Project roles](/dashboard/roles#project-roles) for how project roles combine with organization roles.

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the member's row, open the actions menu and select **Edit role**.
3. Under the project's name, choose a **Role**. To keep the member out of the project, choose **No access**. To remove a project role, choose the organization role again.
4. Click **Save**.

### Transfer ownership

If your organization uses [expanded roles](/dashboard/roles#expanded-roles), the owner can make another member the owner. Only the owner can see the **Owner** role. The previous owner becomes an admin, and only the new owner can transfer ownership again.

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the member's row, open the actions menu and select **Edit role**.
3. Under **Organization**, choose **Owner** as the **Role**, then click **Save**.
4. In the **Transfer ownership?** dialog, type `TRANSFER` to confirm.
5. Click **Transfer ownership**.

You can only transfer ownership to an active member. You can't transfer it to a pending invite or to a member whose role comes from your identity provider.

### Assign a custom role

If your organization has [custom roles](/dashboard/roles#custom-roles), they appear below the built-in roles wherever you choose a role: when you invite members, change a pending invite's role, or change a member's role with **Edit role**. Only the roles whose permissions you have appear.

## Delete a custom role

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the **Custom roles** section, open the role's actions menu and select **Delete role**.
3. If members have the role, choose a new role for each of them. Members you don't change become viewers.
4. Click **Delete permanently**.

If a member gets the role from your identity provider, you can't delete it. Remove the role assignment in your identity provider first.

## Remove a member

Admins can remove a member from the organization entirely, which deletes their access and any keys they created.

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the member's row, open the actions menu and select **Remove member**.
3. If the dialog shows a warning such as **2 API keys will stop working**, click the warning to see each admin and assistant API key the member created. Replace any key your integrations still use before you continue.
4. Click **Remove** to confirm.

The warning leaves out expired keys, which removal also deletes. On Enterprise plans, Mintlify records an [audit log entry](/dashboard/audit-logs#deleted-api-keys) for each deleted key.

You can't remove the owner of an organization that uses [expanded roles](/dashboard/roles#expanded-roles). To remove them, [transfer ownership](#transfer-ownership) to another member first.

You can't remove a member whose row shows **Managed by SSO** because [SCIM directory sync](/dashboard/scim) provisioned them. Remove them from the Mintlify app in your identity provider instead, which removes them from your organization and deletes their keys.

Removing a member deletes the member from your organization. Revoking a member's credentials signs them out and clears their keys, but keeps their membership.

## Revoke a member's credentials {#revoke-a-members-credentials}

Admins can sign a member out of the dashboard and revoke their keys and tokens without removing them from the organization. Use credential revocation when offboarding someone or responding to a compromised account.

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the member's row, open the actions menu and select **Revoke credentials**.
3. Select which credentials to revoke.
4. Click **Revoke**.

You can revoke any combination of the following credentials:

| Credential | What revoking it does |
| ---------- | --------------------- |
| Sessions | Signs the member out of the dashboard. |
| CLI tokens | Signs the member out of the [CLI](/cli/install). |
| API keys | Deletes the API keys that the member created. |
| Discovery API keys | Deletes the discovery API keys that the member created. |
| M2M clients | Revokes the member's machine-to-machine client credentials. |
| Connected apps | Disconnects the apps the member authorized through OAuth. |

Revoking credentials cannot be undone. The member can sign in again and create new keys and tokens unless you also remove them from the organization or block their sign-in method. You cannot revoke your own credentials.
