<Info>
  Role-based access control (RBAC) is available on [Enterprise plans](https://mintlify.com/pricing?ref=rbac).
</Info>

Mintlify provides three dashboard access levels: viewer, editor, and admin. Without RBAC, every member has full access to your organization's dashboard, and new members join as editors.

To invite members, change their roles, transfer ownership, or remove them, see [Members](/dashboard/members).

<Note>
  If the **Role** dropdown on your [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page offers **Publisher**, your organization uses [expanded roles](#expanded-roles) instead.
</Note>

## Expanded roles

Organizations with expanded roles have six roles: owner, admin, publisher, editor, commenter, and viewer.

You can only invite members or assign roles up to your own role.

- **Owner**: Everything an admin can do, plus deleting the project, deleting the organization, and transferring ownership. Every organization has one owner, starting with the person who creates it.
- **Admin**: Full control over content, members, roles, and settings, including billing and the custom domain. Admins can publish without review, unless your deployment branch has branch protection.
- **Publisher**: Everything an editor can do, plus publishing changes and approving [review requests](/editor/review#require-review-before-publishing).
- **Editor**: Create and edit content, comment, request reviews, and manage site and workspace settings, integrations, API keys, and the assistant. Editors can't publish or approve reviews.
- **Commenter**: Everything a viewer can do, plus commenting on pages.
- **Viewer**: View content and preview unpublished edits.

## Custom roles

If none of the built-in roles are sufficient for your team, create a custom role with the exact permissions a member needs. Custom roles are available to organizations with [expanded roles](#expanded-roles). Owners and admins can manage custom roles in the **Custom roles** section on the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page.

### Create a custom role

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the **Custom roles** section, click **New custom role**.
3. Enter a **Label**, which appears in role menus, and an optional description.
4. Select permissions, or select **Grant all permissions**.
5. Click **Create role**.

Permissions come in three groups:

- **Content**: **Pages** has read and write access. **Comments** and **Publishing and reviews** have write access only. Both require **Pages** read access.
- **Project**: View or manage access to project settings, such as **Domain setup**, **Site**, **Editor**, **Git**, and **Assistant**.
- **Organization**: View or manage access to organization settings, such as **Members**, **Security**, **Billing**, and **API keys**.

You can only grant permissions you have yourself. Permissions that only the owner has, such as deleting the organization or transferring ownership, aren't available for custom roles. Every custom role can view the organization, the project, its publish history, and its members.

To change a custom role, open its actions menu in the **Custom roles** section and select **Manage role**.

## Roles on specific projects

If your organization has more than one project, you can give a member a role with more access on specific projects than their organization role. For example, a viewer in your organization can be an editor on one project. A project role only raises access on that project. It never lowers access and doesn't apply to your other projects.

1. Navigate to the [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members) page of your dashboard.
2. In the member's row, open the actions menu and select **Edit role**. The **Edit access and permissions** drawer opens.
3. Under **Organization**, choose the member's organization role.
4. Each project's **Role** starts at the organization role. For each project you want to change, choose a higher role.
5. Click **Save**.

Roles that the organization role already includes are unavailable for a project. If you raise the organization role so it includes a project role, Mintlify removes that project role. Owners and admins already have full access to every project, so you can't set project roles for them. You can only give project roles whose permissions you have. You can't change your own project roles.

The **Role** column on the Members page shows each member's role on the project you have open.

## Repository contributors and deployments

Anyone with write access to your documentation repository can contribute by pushing changes. Pushes to your deploy branch deploy automatically, whatever the person's dashboard role. Dashboard roles only control what people can do in the Mintlify dashboard.
