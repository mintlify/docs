Star Wars is a science-fiction franchise created by George Lucas. Its stories take place in a fictional galaxy of planets, species, droids, and starships. The central conflict is the struggle between the light and dark sides of the Force.

## The galaxy

The Star Wars galaxy connects thousands of worlds through hyperspace travel. The Galactic Republic, the Galactic Empire, the New Republic, and the First Order are among the governments and factions that shape its history.

Stories often move between busy city planets, remote desert worlds, forest moons, and vast space stations. Each setting adds its own cultures, conflicts, and communities to the wider galaxy.

## The Force

The Force is an energy field that connects living things. Force-sensitive characters can learn to sense and influence it.

- **Jedi** serve the light side and train to protect others, resolve conflicts, and use lightsabers.
- **Sith** follow the dark side and pursue power through fear, anger, and control.
- **Lightsabers** are energy blades used by Jedi, Sith, and other trained combatants.

The Force is not limited to Jedi and Sith. Other traditions and individuals understand or use it in different ways.

## Yoda

Yoda is an ancient Jedi Master known for his wisdom, patience, and distinctive speech. He trains generations of Jedi and helps Luke Skywalker understand the discipline required to use the Force. Yoda's long life gives him a broad view of the Jedi Order, its mistakes, and the choices that shape the galaxy.

## The main story

The central saga follows the Skywalker family across three generations:

1. **The prequel trilogy** follows Anakin Skywalker's rise from an enslaved child to a Jedi Knight and his fall to the dark side.
2. **The original trilogy** follows Luke Skywalker's growth from a farm boy into a Jedi who challenges the Galactic Empire.
3. **The sequel trilogy** follows Rey, Finn, and Poe as they join the Resistance and confront the First Order.

Standalone films and television series expand the setting with stories about characters, communities, and events beyond the Skywalker family.

## Where to begin

The nine-episode Skywalker Saga provides the broadest introduction to the franchise. The original trilogy begins with *A New Hope*, while the prequel and sequel trilogies provide earlier and later chapters of the story.

For stories outside the main saga, *Rogue One* explores the events leading to *A New Hope*, and *The Mandalorian* follows a bounty hunter and a Force-sensitive child after the fall of the Empire.

## Why Star Wars endures

Star Wars combines space adventure with themes of family, friendship, identity, redemption, and resistance to tyranny. Its stories invite you to explore both the spectacle of the galaxy and the choices that shape its characters.
