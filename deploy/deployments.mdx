Your documentation site automatically deploys when you push changes to your connected repository. If your repository is on GitHub, you must install and connect the Mintlify GitHub App. If your repository is on GitLab or Bitbucket, see [GitLab](/deploy/gitlab) or [Bitbucket](/deploy/bitbucket) for connection setup.

If your latest changes are not appearing on your live site, work through these checks in order:

1. Open the [activity](https://app.mintlify.com/activity) page in your dashboard and confirm a build was triggered for your latest commit. If the build failed, expand it to see the error.
2. Verify that you pushed to the branch configured as your deployment branch in [Git Settings](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/deployment/git-settings). Pushes to other branches don't deploy.
3. If your repository is on GitHub, confirm the GitHub account or organization that owns the docs repository still has the Mintlify GitHub App installed. See [GitHub troubleshooting](/deploy/github#troubleshooting) for the full reset flow.

If your repository is connected and the deployment branch is correct but no build was triggered, manually trigger a deployment from your dashboard.

## Manually trigger a deployment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your latest commit was successful.">
    Check that your latest commit appears in your docs repository and did not encounter any errors. You can confirm whether the commit built successfully in your [deployment history](#view-deployment-history).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manually trigger a deployment.">
    Go to your [dashboard](https://app.mintlify.com) and click the deploy button.

## View deployment history

Navigate to the [activity](https://app.mintlify.com/activity) page in your dashboard to review your deployment history and check whether each build succeeded. On the **Live** tab, click a build to expand it and view details, including the commit message and author.

## Roll back a deployment

If a change reaches your live site that you must remove immediately, serve a previous successful build instead of reverting commits and waiting for a new build. See [Rollbacks](/deploy/rollbacks).

## Delete a deployment

You can permanently delete a deployment from the **Danger zone** section of the [Organization](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/general) settings page in your dashboard. This action is irreversible and removes all deployment data, including any associated preview deployments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Danger zone.">
    Go to the **Danger zone** section of the [Organization](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/general) settings page in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the deployment.">
{/* vale Mintlify.FirstPerson = NO */}

    1. In the **Delete my deployment** section, provide a reason for deletion.
    2. Click the delete button and confirm that you want to delete the deployment.

{/* vale Mintlify.FirstPerson = YES */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a deployment is permanent and cannot be undone. If you have an active subscription, you'll receive a prorated credit for any unused time remaining in your billing period.
</Warning>

If you have multiple deployments, Mintlify redirects you to another deployment after deletion. If you delete your only deployment, Mintlify redirects you to the Mintlify homepage. When you delete a deployment, the organization admin receives an email notification confirming the deletion.
