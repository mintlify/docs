Private authentication restricts your site to members of your Mintlify organization. Readers sign in with their Mintlify account. Use it for internal documentation that only your team reads.

To give people outside your organization access, use [emails authentication](/deploy/emails-authentication) instead.

## Prerequisites

- Everyone who needs to read your site is a member of your Mintlify organization, or you can [add them as members](/dashboard/members).

## Set up private authentication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on private authentication.">
    1. In your dashboard, go to [Access](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/project/authentication).
    2. Set **Visibility** to **Private**.
    3. Set **Method** to **Authenticated**.
    4. Click **Save**.

    After you save, your site redeploys. When it finishes deploying, anyone who visits your site must sign in to your Mintlify organization to read your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add readers as members.">
    1. In your dashboard, go to [Members](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/members).
    2. Add each person who should have access to your documentation.
    3. Assign each person a [role](/dashboard/roles) that matches what they need to do in your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To keep some pages readable without signing in, [make them public](/deploy/authentication-setup#make-pages-public).
