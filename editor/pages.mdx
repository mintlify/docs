## Sidebar sections

The editor sidebar groups your pages into sections.

- **Navigation**: The pages and groups in your site navigation, as defined in `docs.json`.
- **Files**: Every file in your repository that isn't in your navigation, including images, videos, OpenAPI specs, and `docs.json`.
- **Recents**: The last three private pages you opened.
- **Starred**: Private pages you starred. To star a page, open its options menu and select **Star**. Stars are personal to you.
- **Personal**: Your [private pages](#private-pages).
- **Shared with me**: Pages that teammates [shared with you](#share-a-private-page) directly.

Personal, Shared with me, Recents, and Starred only appear after you create a private page or a teammate shares one with you. Recents, Starred, and Shared with me stay hidden while they're empty.

<Note>
  In a [base](/base/index), the **Navigation** section is called **Teamspace** and **Personal** always appears.
</Note>

Drag a section header to reorder the sections. Click a section header to collapse or expand it. The editor saves your collapsed sections to your account and your section order to your browser.

The top bar changes with the page you have open. On a site page, it has the branch selector, **Preview**, and **Publish**. On a private page, it has **Share**.

## Create a page

To create a page, click the <Icon icon="plus" /> plus button where you want it, then select **New page**.

- **Navigation** section header: Adds a new page to the top of your navigation.
- A group or other navigation element: Adds a new page inside that element.
- **Files** section header or a folder in **Files**: Creates a file that isn't in your navigation.

The **New page** button at the bottom of the sidebar also adds a page to the top of your navigation.

<Tip>
  To add a page that already exists in your repository to your navigation, click the <Icon icon="plus" /> plus button and select **Add existing page**.
</Tip>

The same menus create navigation elements like groups, tabs, anchors, dropdowns, versions, languages, and products. For more information on each element, see [Navigation](/organize/navigation).

## Edit page settings

Set a page's title, description, and icon directly at the top of the page. Hover above the title and click **Add icon** to pick an icon or emoji. When a page with an icon is open, your browser tab shows that icon.

For other settings, open the page's options menu in the sidebar, or click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **More options** in the top-right of the page, and select **Page settings**. The **Page settings** panel groups settings into sections:

- **URL**: The page's path and redirect URL. Changing the path moves the file.
- **Navigation**: Sidebar title, tag, mode, and whether the page is hidden.
- **SEO & sharing**: Open Graph image and keywords.
- **Thumbnails**: [Thumbnail overrides](/organize/settings-appearance#override-thumbnails-for-a-page-or-section) for the page's social preview image. Each setting uses the inherited value unless you change it.

Groups, tabs, and other navigation elements have their own settings panels, like **Group settings** and **Tab settings**. Open them from the element's options menu. Groups, tabs, anchors, dropdowns, products, versions, languages, and menu items also have a **Thumbnails** section that sets thumbnail overrides for the pages in that section. See [Pages](/organize/pages) for more information on specific settings.

## Verify a page

In a [base](/base/index), each page shows a **Status** row below its description. The status tells your team whether someone has confirmed that the page is accurate.

- **Not verified**: Nobody has verified the page.
- **Verified**: A teammate confirmed the page is accurate.
- **Outdated**: A teammate flagged the page as wrong, so readers shouldn't rely on it yet.
- **Expired**: The page was verified, but the verification period has ended.

To change the status, click the status badge and select **Verified**, **Outdated**, or **Not verified**. When you select **Verified**, choose how long the verification lasts: 90, 30, or 7 days, indefinitely, or until a date you pick. Then click **Verify**, **Mark outdated**, or **Clear status** to save.

After you save, the row shows when the status changed and who changed it. If you can't edit the page, you can see its status but not change it.

## Edit API reference pages

Pages with an `openapi`, `openapi-schema`, or `api` field in their frontmatter show the endpoint and its fields in the editor, the same way they appear on your published site. The editor labels these sections **Generated from OpenAPI**. You can't edit them in the editor. To change them, update your OpenAPI specification or the page's frontmatter. See [OpenAPI setup](/api-playground/openapi-setup).

Write your own content on the page between the endpoint and the fields.

If the page has no `description` in its frontmatter, the editor shows the operation's description from your OpenAPI specification. Click the description to edit it. If you change it, the editor saves your text as the page's `description`, which replaces the specification's description on that page.

## Add media

Type <kbd>/image</kbd> or <kbd>/video</kbd> to upload a file or pick one already in your repository. You can also drag files from your computer onto the page, or drag an image or video from the **Files** section onto the page.

- Uploads save to your `images/`, `videos/`, and `files/` folders and commit with your next publish. Before you insert an upload, you can edit its file path.
- You can upload PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, ICO, and BMP images, MP4, WebM, and MOV videos, and PDF files. You can't upload SVG files in the editor.
- Each upload can be up to 20 MB.
- Click an image to select it. Drag the image to move it, including into or out of components like frames, cards, tabs, and callouts. Drag the handles on its edges to resize it. Press <kbd>Delete</kbd> or <kbd>Backspace</kbd> to remove it.
- To serve a different image in dark mode, select the image, click **Edit Image**, and set **Dark mode image**.
- Type <kbd>/embed</kbd> to add an embed. On the **Video link** tab, paste a YouTube, Loom, or Vimeo URL, or a direct link to a video file. On the **Embed code** tab, paste an iframe or other embed snippet.

For formatting images and using the Frame component, see [Images and embeds](/create/image-embeds).

## Organize navigation

Drag elements in **Navigation** to reorder or nest them. The editor writes your changes to `docs.json` when you publish.

<Note>
  Some elements can't nest inside others. Tabs can't nest inside groups, for example. The editor prevents invalid combinations.
</Note>

To select several items, <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click to select a range, or <kbd>Cmd</kbd>-click (macOS) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click (Windows) to add or remove individual items. Drag the selection or right-click it to move, duplicate, or delete every selected item at once. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to clear the selection.

To change a group or other element into a different type, open its options menu and select **Convert to**.

Global navigation elements, like anchors and tabs that appear across every section of your site, aren't in the sidebar tree. To manage them, open a site page, click **Site configuration** at the bottom of the sidebar, then go to **Navigation**.

### Add and remove pages from your navigation

Drag a page from **Files** into **Navigation** to add it at that position. Drag a folder to add it as a group. You can also select **Move to navigation** from a file's options menu.

To remove a page or group from your navigation without deleting its files, drag it from **Navigation** into **Files**, or select **Move to files** from its options menu. An undo toast appears after the move.

## Files

Click a file in **Files** to open it.

- Images open in a preview. Click the image to zoom.
- Videos play in the editor.
- `docs.json` opens in source mode.
- Text and code files, like YAML, JSON, and CSS, open in the source editor.
- The editor can't preview other files, like fonts and archives.

To upload files, drag them from your computer onto the **Files** section.

## Move a page

To move a page, open its options menu and select **Move**, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> (macOS) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> (Windows) with the page open.

You can open a page's options menu from:

- <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **More options** in the top-right of an open page.
- The <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> menu next to a page in the sidebar.
- Right-clicking a page in the sidebar.

In the picker, search to filter destinations, expand folders and groups to see nested destinations, and select a destination to move the page.

What the picker offers depends on where the item is in the sidebar:

- **Navigation**: Pages and groups move to a different group in your navigation. Moving a group moves its pages with it.
- **Files**: Pages move their file to a different folder.
- **Personal**: Private pages and folders move to another private folder, or into **Navigation** to add them to your site. See [Move a page to your site](#move-a-page-to-your-site).

Editors and admins can move site pages. You can move private pages and folders in **Personal**, but not pages in **Shared with me**.

## Copy a page to another project

To copy a page to another project in your organization, open its options menu and select **Copy to a project**, then pick the project. You must have an editor or admin role and access to both projects.

## Delete a page

To delete a page, click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **More options** in the top-right of the page, then click **Move to trash**. You can also delete a page from its options menu in the sidebar.

Deleting a site page removes both the file and its navigation entry. A toast with **Undo** appears after you delete. To remove a page from your navigation without deleting its file, select **Move to files** instead.

Deleting a private page is permanent. For private pages, the **More options** menu shows **Delete page**.

Editors and admins can delete site pages.

## Version history

Version history lets you compare a page with earlier saved versions and restore one. It isn't available for `docs.json`.

The editor records a new version about 10 seconds after you stop editing a page. If you edit continuously, the editor records a version at least once a minute. Changes that sync from your Git repository also create versions, which list **Git sync** as an author. Version history is branch-scoped. Only the current branch's version history is available.

1. Click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **More options** in the top-right of the page.
1. Click **Version history**.
1. Select a version. The editor shows how the current page differs from that version.
1. To restore it, click **Restore this version**.

To leave the comparison, click **Exit comparison**.

Anyone can compare versions. Editors and admins can restore versions of site pages. For private pages, you need the **Editor** or **Full Access** role on the page to restore a version.

Restoring a version replaces the page's current content. Restoring a site page only affects your current branch. Restoring a private page affects all branches.

## Private pages

Private pages are drafts that live in the **Personal** section of the sidebar. They stay private to you, and Mintlify doesn't commit them to your repository. They save automatically and have no publish step. They never appear on your published site unless you move them to your site.

If you already use private pages, create a page or folder by clicking the <Icon icon="plus" /> plus button on the **Personal** section header, then selecting **New page** or **New folder**. On sites, **Personal** only appears for people who have private pages or have had one shared with them.

To import Markdown files as private pages, drag Markdown and MDX files from your computer onto **Personal** or a private folder.

To set an icon or emoji on a private folder, open its options menu and select **Change icon**. Pick an icon from the **Icons** tab or an emoji from the **Emojis** tab, or select **Remove** to clear it.

### Share a private page

To share a private page, open it and click **Share** in the top bar. To share a folder, open its options menu in the sidebar and select **Share**. Search for teammates and select a role for each:

- **Full Access**: Edit and share. The page's owner can also move it to your site.
- **Editor**: Create and edit content.
- **Viewer**: View content only.

Under general access, select **Only people invited** or **Everyone at** your organization. Only people with **Full Access** can change who has access.

If you have **Viewer** access to a page, click **View only** in the top bar, then **Request edit access**. People with **Full Access** can approve the request.

Pages shared with you appear in **Shared with me**. When a teammate shares a page or folder with you directly, it also appears in your [inbox](/dashboard/inbox). To remove a page from your sidebar, open its options menu and select **Leave**.

## Move a page to your site

To add a private page to your site, open its options menu in the sidebar, select **Move**, and choose **Navigation** or a group inside it. You can also drag the page into **Navigation**. You need the editor or admin role.

The page moves into your navigation on your current branch as an unpublished change, and the sidebar highlights it. [Publish](/editor/publish) to make it live.

Moving a folder adds it to your navigation as a group, with all its pages and its icon. You can move up to 20 pages at a time.

<Warning>
  Moving a page to your site is one-way. The private page is removed right away, and discarding the change on your branch doesn't restore it. You can't move a site page back to **Personal**.
</Warning>
