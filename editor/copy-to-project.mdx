---
title: "Copy a page to another project"
sidebarTitle: "Copy to a project"
description: "Copy a page from one project to another project in your organization from the editor."
keywords: ["editor", "copy", "project", "pages", "duplicate"]
---

To copy a page to another project in your organization, open its options menu and select **Copy to a project**, then pick the project. The editor switches to that project and opens the copied page.

You need the editor or admin role and access to at least one other project.
