Preview your changes before they reach your live site, ask teammates to approve them, and publish or merge them from the editor.

## Live preview

Live preview renders your site as you edit, with no build step. With a site page open, click <Icon icon="play" /> **Preview** in the top bar. The preview opens full screen. Click **Refresh preview** to reload it, or **Close preview** to return to the editor. To open the preview in a new browser tab instead, turn on [Open live preview in new tab](/editor/settings#appearance).

To edit site settings and see how they affect your content, click **Site settings** in the preview toolbar, then select a configuration section.

To open the [agent](/editor/agent) beside the preview, click **Ask agent** in the preview toolbar. Only editors and admins can use the agent in live previews.

<Note>
  Live preview isn't available for sites that use standalone [personalization](/create/personalization) without authentication.
</Note>

## Preview deployments

When your feature branch has a pull request, Mintlify builds a preview deployment: a temporary URL where your changes render exactly as they look when published. The preview rebuilds each time the editor commits new changes to the branch. The URL combines your project's subdomain and the branch name: `<subdomain>-<branch-name>.mintlify.site`. Find the exact URL in the pull request comment from the Mintlify bot.

Preview URLs are publicly accessible by default. To restrict access to members of your Mintlify organization, enable preview authentication in the **Previews** section of the [Site](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/project/site) page in your dashboard. See [Restrict access to preview deployments](/deploy/preview-deployments#restrict-access-to-preview-deployments) for more information.

## Which preview to use

| Feature | Live preview | Preview deployment |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Instant | Requires a build |
| **Access** | Inside the editor | Shareable URL |
| **Use when** | Reviewing changes while editing | Sharing with your team for review |
| **Availability** | While the editor is open | As long as the branch exists |

## Share an editor link

To point a teammate at a specific page on a branch, click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> **More options** in the top-right of the page, then click **Copy link**. Anyone with access to your Mintlify organization can open the link in their own editor session.

## Require review before publishing

<Info>
  Require review before publishing is available on [Enterprise plans](https://mintlify.com/pricing?ref=review).
</Info>

If an admin turns on [Require review before publishing](/editor/settings#require-review-before-publishing), changes on your deployment branch need a teammate's approval before they go live. Editors request a review instead of publishing. A reviewer approves the changes in the editor, then someone who can publish makes them live.

Require review is only available if your repository is on GitHub.

Reviews in the editor happen on your deployment branch. You can still work on other branches, but only admins can merge pull requests into your deployment branch from the editor. Everyone else merges pull requests in GitHub. See [Review and merge pull requests](#review-and-merge-pull-requests).

What each [role](/dashboard/roles) can do depends on whether your organization has the publisher role.

| Role | Request a review | Approve changes | Publish approved changes | Publish without review |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Owner and admin | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Publisher | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Editor, if your organization has the publisher role | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Editor, if your organization doesn't have the publisher role | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Commenter | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Viewer | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

Reviewers can also leave [comments and suggestions](/editor/collaborate) on changes in review. Comments and suggestions don't approve or block a review.

### Request a review

1. Make your changes on your deployment branch.
1. Click **Request review** in the top bar. This button replaces **Publish** when your changes need review.
1. In the **Request review** section, click **Choose reviewers** and select people or a group. You can only choose members who can approve changes: admins and publishers, or admins and editors if your organization doesn't have the publisher role.
1. Click **Request**.

The request includes every page you changed that isn't already in review. Each reviewer gets an email and an item in their [inbox](/dashboard/inbox).

Admins can publish without review, but they can also request a review from the **Publish** menu.

### Review and approve changes

1. Open the review request from your inbox or the email, or open a page in the request.
1. Read the changes. To leave comments or suggestions instead of approving, use the [comment and suggestion tools](/editor/collaborate).
1. Click **Approve** in the banner at the top of the page. Your approval covers every page in the request.

You can't approve your own request. To leave review mode, click **Reviewing changes** in the top bar.

### Publish approved changes

When a request is approved, the requester gets an inbox item, and admins and publishers can publish the changes.

1. Open a page in the approved request.
1. Click **Publish** in the top bar. The **Publish** menu lists the requester's changes.
1. Click **Publish** followed by the requester's name, such as **Publish Alex's changes**, then click **Publish** to confirm.

The approved pages publish along with the requester's navigation and configuration changes, and the request closes. If another open request includes an unapproved page that changes your navigation, navigation and `docs.json` changes stay unpublished until that page is approved.

If you're a publisher and requested a review of your own changes, click **Publish** after they're approved. The **Publish** menu groups your changes into **Approved** and **Not requested yet**. Click **Publish approved changes** to publish the approved ones.

### Edit a page after approval

An approval covers the version of a page that the reviewer saw. If you edit, rename, or move an approved page, or change its place in the navigation, the approval no longer applies.

When you start editing an approved page, the editor asks you to confirm. Click **Make change** to edit the page and dismiss the approval. If the page is part of a request with other approved pages, the editor dismisses the approvals on all of them.

After an approved page changes, the requester sees a banner on the page that says it was edited after approval. The requester clicks **Request review again** to ask the same reviewers to approve the new version.

### Withdraw a review request

To withdraw your request before anyone approves it, open a page in the request and click **Withdraw** in the banner.

To remove one reviewer, open the **Publish** menu and click **Withdraw** next to the reviewer's name. If you remove the last reviewer, the request closes.

If you discard all the changes in a request, the request closes.

## Review and merge pull requests

When your branch has a pull request, the publish menu shows its changed files and review status, like **Pull request created**, **Review required**, **Changes requested**, or **Approved**. If Mintlify hosts your repository, a draft shows **Draft in progress**. If your repository is on GitHub or GitLab, click **View request** to open the pull request in your Git provider.

<Tip>
  If the branch has unresolved comment threads, the publish menu shows a warning with the number of open comments in place of **Ready to publish** or **Approved**. You can still merge and publish. The warning reminds you to resolve feedback first.
</Tip>

On a feature branch, reviewers approve your changes in your Git provider, not in the editor. If your project [requires review before publishing](#require-review-before-publishing), only admins can merge pull requests into your deployment branch from the editor.

1. Click **Publish** in the top bar, then click **Request review**. This marks the pull request ready for review.
1. Reviewers approve the pull request in your Git provider.
1. Click **Publish**, then click **Merge and publish**. The editor merges the pull request, deploys your changes, and switches you back to your deployment branch.

If Mintlify hosts your repository and approvals aren't required, the publish menu shows **Publish** instead, which merges and deploys in one step.

<Tip>
  Configure branch protection rules in your Git provider to require pull requests. See [About protected branches](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-protected-branches/about-protected-branches) in the GitHub help or [Protected branches](https://docs.gitlab.com/user/project/repository/branches/protected/) in the GitLab help.
</Tip>

### Compare changes before merging

Click any changed file in the publish menu to open it in diff view against the published version. To leave diff view, click **Viewing changes** in the top bar. Visual mode shows a visual diff and source mode shows a text diff. Files that can't display a diff, such as images or deleted files, appear in the list but aren't clickable.

You can open [hidden pages](/organize/hidden-pages) that appear in the branch diff from the publish menu, even though they don't appear in your site navigation.

### Pull request titles and descriptions

The pull request title comes from your branch title.

If your branch has open comment threads, Mintlify adds a summary of them to the pull request description. See [Collaborate](/editor/collaborate#comments).

## Review an automation run

When an [automation](/automations) opens a pull request, click **View changes** in the Slack notification or notification email to open the editor on the automation's branch. The editor opens in diff view, and the [agent](/editor/agent#continue-an-automation-run) opens alongside it with context on what the automation did.

To open the automation's preview deployment instead, open the run from the **Runs** tab on the [Automations](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/project/automations?tab=runs) page of your dashboard, then click **Preview**.
