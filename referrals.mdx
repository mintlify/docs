Refer a team to Mintlify and you both earn credits. When a team that you refer subscribes to the Pro plan, they get $500 in credits and you get $500 in credits. Your organization can earn up to three rewards, for a maximum of $1,500 in credits.

Manage your referrals on the [Referrals](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/referrals) page in your dashboard.

## Plan availability

The referral program is available on Pro and Growth plans. Enterprise plans do not include the referral program. On other plans, the Referrals page prompts you to upgrade to Pro.

## Refer a team

You can refer a team by email or by sharing your referral link.

### Send email invitations

1. Navigate to the [Referrals](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/referrals) page in your dashboard.
2. Enter up to 10 email addresses, separated by commas.
3. Select **Send Invite**.

Mintlify emails each person an invitation that contains your referral link. If Mintlify skips an address, the page shows the reason: the email is invalid, the person is already invited, you entered your own address, or delivery failed. You can send as many invitations as you want. Only rewards are capped.

### Share your referral link

Copy your referral link from the **Your referral link** field on the [Referrals](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/referrals) page and share it anywhere.

When someone opens your link, Mintlify stores your referral code in their browser for 30 days and directs them to signup. If they create an account within that window, the signup counts toward your referrals.

## Track referral activity

The activity table on the Referrals page lists each referral with its status. The table refreshes automatically while the page is open.

| Status | Meaning |
|:-------|:--------|
| **Invited** | You sent an invitation or the person opened your link, but they have not created an account yet. |
| **Signed up** | The person created a Mintlify account. |
| **Completed** | The referred team subscribed to Pro. Both organizations receive $500 in credits. |

The **Earnings** column shows the reward that each completed referral granted. You can delete a referral only while its status is **Invited**. After the person creates an account, you can no longer remove the referral.

## Rewards

Each completed referral adds $500 in credits to your organization and $500 in credits to the referred organization. Credits power the assistant, the agent, and automations. See [Credit pricing](/credits) for how credits work.
