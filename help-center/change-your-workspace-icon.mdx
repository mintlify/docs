Your organization icon is the image that represents your organization in the Mintlify dashboard. If no icon is set, the dashboard displays the first letter of your organization name instead.

Changing the icon requires permission to update the organization. Anyone with access to the organization can view the settings page, but the icon controls are disabled without update permission. If the controls are disabled, contact an admin of your organization. See [Roles](/dashboard/roles).

## Upload or replace the icon

1. Go to the [General](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/general) page in your organization settings.
2. Next to **Icon**, select **Upload**. If an icon is already set, select **Replace**.
3. Select a PNG, JPEG, WebP, or SVG file that is 4 MB or smaller.

The dashboard shows the new icon as soon as the upload completes.

## Remove the icon

On the same [General](https://app.mintlify.com/settings/organization/general) page, click the **Remove organization icon** button next to the current icon. The dashboard falls back to displaying the first letter of your organization name.

## What your readers see

The organization icon only appears in the dashboard. The logo displayed on your documentation site comes from the [`logo` field](/organize/settings-reference#logo) in `docs.json`.
