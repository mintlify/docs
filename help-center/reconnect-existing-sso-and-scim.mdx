If your organization set up [SSO](/dashboard/sso) or [SCIM](/dashboard/scim) before the admin portal was available, an admin must reconnect your identity provider through the admin portal. Your existing SSO setup keeps working until your organization switches over. SCIM set up before the admin portal no longer adds or removes members, so reconnect SCIM as soon as you can to keep members in sync with your identity provider.

## Reconnect your identity provider

1. In the admin portal, follow the steps for your identity provider.
2. Add the new connection as a new application in your identity provider. Keep your existing Mintlify application so members can still sign in until your organization switches over.

## When your organization switches over

When your organization switches over, Mintlify signs every member out of the dashboard. Members sign in again through your identity provider.

After the switch, delete the old Mintlify application from your identity provider.
