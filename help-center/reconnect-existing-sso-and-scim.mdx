If your organization set up [SSO](/dashboard/sso) or [SCIM](/dashboard/scim) before the admin portal was available, an admin must reconnect your identity provider by November 20, 2026. Your existing setup keeps working until then.

When your organization needs to reconnect, the dashboard shows a banner that names what to reconnect: SSO, SCIM, or both.

## Reconnect your identity provider

1. In the banner, click **Set up now**. Only members who can manage SSO see this button.
2. In the admin portal, follow the steps for your identity provider.
3. Add the new connection as a new application in your identity provider. Keep your existing Mintlify application so members can still sign in until your organization switches over.

If SSO and SCIM are already reconnected, Mintlify shows **SSO and SCIM are already set up.** instead of opening the admin portal.

## When your organization switches over

When your organization switches over, Mintlify signs every member out of the dashboard. Members sign in again through your identity provider.

After the switch, delete the old Mintlify application from your identity provider.
